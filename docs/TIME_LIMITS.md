# Time limit for every level (generated from levels/levels.json, 5 October 2026)

Rule today: the first block of 5 levels gets the start time, then +5 seconds for every further block of 5. Quick starts at 20s, Classic at 55s, Master at 180s.

`s/gap` is the time limit divided by the number of gaps to fill. A drop takes two taps (pick a number, tap a column), so about 1.5 to 2 seconds is the floor even for an expert; most players need 3 to 6.
