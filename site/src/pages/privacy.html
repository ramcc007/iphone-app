iCloud

If iCloud is turned on for the app, the data above is saved to your own iCloud account using Apple’s iCloud key-value storage, so it syncs between your iPhone and iPad. That copy belongs to your Apple ID and is handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. It is not accessible to us. You can turn it off in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising in the app, and no advertising identifier (IDFA): the app never asks to track you.
  • No tracking of you across other companies’ apps and websites, and nothing is used to show you personalised ads.
  • Your name, progress and session history are never sent to us or to Google.
  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No sale of data.
  • We run no server. Network traffic is Apple’s own iCloud and Game Center if you use them, and Google Analytics only if you allow ad measurement (see below).

Ad measurement (optional, from version 1.2)

We advertise {{appName}} with Google Ads. To see which ads bring new players, version 1.2 and later include Google Analytics for Firebase. It is off until you choose: the app asks once (“Help NumFall find new players?”) with two equal buttons, and nothing is collected unless you tap Allow.

  • What is shared if you allow it: a random identifier for this installation of the app (not your Apple ID and not the advertising identifier), app activity such as the first time the app opens, finishing the tutorial and clearing a level (the board and stars), basic device information (model, iOS version, app version) and an approximate location (country or region) that Google works out from your IP address.
  • What is never shared: your name, your session history, your iCloud data, or anything you type.
  • Why: to count installs and played levels that come from our own ads, and to understand how many people play (analytics). It is not used to show you ads, and ad personalisation is turned off.
  • Who processes it: Google, under the Google Privacy Policy and Google’s data processing terms. Google may process it outside your country.
  • How long: we keep Analytics data for 2 months.
  • Your choice: change it any time in the app: Settings → Privacy → “Share anonymous ad measurement”. Turning it off stops collection and resets the app’s analytics identifier.

Game Center (optional)

If you are signed in to Game Center on your device, NumFall can send Apple your achievements, your total number of stars and your longest Daily Drop streak so they appear in Game Center and on its leaderboards. This is handled entirely by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy: leaderboards show your Game Center nickname and score to other players, and we do not receive any of it. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent and the game works the same.

Daily reminder (optional)

If you turn it on, NumFall asks iOS for permission to show a notification and schedules a short reminder on your own device (about 7 pm, for the next two weeks, skipped on days you have already played). It is a local notification: no server or account is involved and we never learn whether it is on. You can turn it off in NumFall’s Settings or in iOS Settings → Notifications.

Information from Apple

If you choose to share app analytics or crash reports with developers in your iOS settings, Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated statistics (for example, crash counts). They do not identify you.

Links

This website (numfall.store)

If you tap Accept on the cookie notice, this website uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful. It never runs inside the app. If you tap No thanks, Google Analytics is not loaded at all.

  • What Google Analytics records: the pages you view, the website that sent you here, your approximate location (country or city, from your IP address, which Google does not store), your device type, browser and screen size, and how long you stay. It uses first-party cookies named _ga to recognise a returning browser.
  • Why: to understand how many people visit and which pages help them, so we can improve the site. It is not used for advertising and is not linked to the app.
  • Who processes it: Google, under the Google Privacy Policy. Google may process it outside your country.
  • How long: we keep analytics data for 2 months.
  • Your choice: change it any time with “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page, or by clearing this site’s cookies. You can also use Google’s opt-out add-on.

Your choice (accept or decline) is remembered in your browser’s local storage.

Web preview

Keeping and deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → “Reset all progress…” → “Delete everything” removes your name, progress and session history from the device and from your iCloud copy.
  • Deleting the app removes the data stored on that device. Delete the iCloud copy as above, or in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.
  • You can change your name at any time in Settings → Player → Edit name.
