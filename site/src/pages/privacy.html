What the app stores

WhatWhyWhere it lives

iCloud

If iCloud is turned on for the app, the data above is saved to your own iCloud account using Apple’s iCloud key-value storage, so it syncs between your iPhone and iPad. That copy belongs to your Apple ID and is handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. It is not accessible to us. You can turn it off in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking of you across other companies’ apps and websites.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting software of our own, and no third-party software development kits (SDKs).
  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No sale or sharing of data, because we do not have any.
  • The game itself makes no network connections.

Information from Apple

If you choose to share app analytics or crash reports with developers in your iOS settings, Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated statistics (for example, crash counts). They do not identify you.

Sharing a result

The Share button creates a short text such as “Classic · Level 12 ★★★” and opens the iOS share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

Links

The Privacy Policy, Terms and Help links in Settings open this website in your browser. Visiting it is subject to this site’s hosting provider’s logs, which we do not use to identify visitors.

Web preview

The web version saves your name and progress in your browser’s local storage on your own device. Nothing is sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Children

{{appName}} is a general-audience puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because the app does not send any information to us. If you are under 13 (or the age required where you live), please ask a parent or guardian before typing your name in the app, and use a first name or nickname instead.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → “Reset all progress…” → “Delete everything” removes your name, progress and session history from the device and from your iCloud copy.
  • Deleting the app removes the data stored on that device. Delete the iCloud copy as above, or in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.
  • You can change your name at any time in Settings → Player → Edit name.

Your rights
