How it works

Easy to learn. Tricky to master.

1

Pick a number

Choose from the tray. Each number can be used only as many times as the grid needs it.

2

Tap a column

The number falls to the lowest empty cell. A ghost tile shows exactly where it will land.

3

Fill the grid

Order matters: lower gaps first. Finish before the clock hits zero for up to three stars.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it free?

Yes. There are no ads and no in-app purchases. Sparks are earned only by playing.

Do I need an account?

No. You just type a name the first time you play. It stays on your device and in your own iCloud.

What happens when the clock runs out?

The level starts again. After two tries you can skip it with Sparks and come back later.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad, in any orientation. It also runs on Mac with Apple silicon and on Apple Vision Pro as an iPad app.
