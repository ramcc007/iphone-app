{{tagline}} Pick a number, drop it into a column, and it falls to the lowest gap. Fill the grid before the clock runs out.

How it works

  • Sudoku rule: every row, column and box holds each number once.
  • Gravity: numbers fall to the lowest empty cell, so the order you play in is the whole puzzle.
  • Beat the clock: every level has a countdown. Early levels are short and tight.
  • Three boards: Quick 4×4 (30 levels), Classic 6×6 (100 levels) and Master 9×9 (100 levels), all open from the start.
  • Earn Sparks by playing and spend them on hints, extra undos or skipping a stuck level.

Private by design

No ads. No purchases. No tracking. No accounts. Your name and progress stay on your device and in your own iCloud. Read the Privacy Policy.
